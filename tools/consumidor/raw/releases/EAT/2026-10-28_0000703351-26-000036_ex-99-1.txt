BRINKER INTERNATIONAL REPORTS FIRST QUARTER FISCAL 2027 RESULTS AND UPDATES FISCAL 2027 GUIDANCE
DALLAS (October 28, 2026) – Brinker International, Inc. (NYSE: EAT) today announced its financial results for the first quarter ended September 23, 2026 and updated guidance for fiscal 2027.
First Quarter Fiscal 2027 Financial Highlights
“Chili’s delivered its 22nd consecutive quarter of same-store sales growth, up +10%, lapping a +21% from last year and a +14% from 2024 for a 3 year cumulative comp of +52%,” said Kevin Hochman, President and CEO of Brinker International. “The Chili’s flywheel of sustainable growth and market share gains keeps spinning, with industry leading value, world class marketing, and continued investments in operations that both attract new guests and keep regular guests coming back.”
In the first quarter of fiscal 2027, Company sales were $1,461.2 million compared to $1,335.4 million in the first quarter of fiscal 2026. Company comparable restaurant sales increased 9.1% in the first quarter of fiscal 2027, including 10.1% for Chili’s, as the brand drove positive traffic and continued outperformance against the casual dining industry. Our first quarter results reflect the continued success of our ongoing brand initiatives to improve the guest experience, further strengthened by the highly successful launch of our Big Crispy® chicken sandwich.
Net cash provided by operating activities was $169.1 million in the first quarter of fiscal 2027, compared to $120.8 million in the first quarter of fiscal 2026, driven primarily by an increase in operating income. Free cash flow, non-GAAP was $105.7 million in the first quarter of fiscal 2027, compared to $62.2 million in the first quarter of fiscal 2026. During the first quarter of fiscal 2027, the Company utilized operational cash flow to repurchase $125.0 million of the Company’s common stock and lowered its cost of capital by redeeming the outstanding $350.0 million 8.25% notes using funding from the revolving credit facility.

Chili’s
•Chili’s Company sales increased primarily due to favorable comparable restaurant sales driven by higher traffic and menu pricing.
•Chili’s Company restaurant expenses, as a percentage of Company sales, decreased primarily due to sales leverage, partially offset by unfavorable commodity costs, higher hourly labor, advertising, repairs and maintenance, worker’s compensation and general liability insurance, delivery fees and to-go supplies, and other restaurant expenses.
•Chili’s franchisees generated sales of approximately $296.7 million for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to $269.5 million for the first quarter of fiscal 2026.
Maggiano’s
•Maggiano’s Company sales decreased primarily due to lower traffic, partially offset by menu pricing.
•Maggiano’s Company restaurant expenses, as a percentage of Company sales, decreased primarily due to lower advertising, delivery fees and to-go supplies, and other restaurant expenses, partially offset by sales deleverage and higher rent and manager salaries.

About Brinker
Brinker International, Inc. is one of the world’s leading casual dining restaurant companies and home of Chili’s® Grill & Bar, and Maggiano’s Little Italy.® Founded in 1975 in Dallas, Texas, we’ve ventured far from home, but stayed true to our roots. Brinker owns, operates or franchises more than 1,600 restaurants in the United States, 28 other countries and two U.S. territories. Our passion is making everyone feel special, and we hope you feel that passion each time you visit one of our restaurants or invite us into your home through takeout or delivery. Learn more about Brinker and its brands at brinker.com.

Restaurant operating margin is not a measurement determined in accordance with GAAP and should not be considered in isolation, or as an alternative to operating income as an indicator of financial performance. Restaurant operating margin is widely regarded in the restaurant industry as a useful metric by which to evaluate restaurant-level operating efficiency and performance of ongoing restaurant-level operations. This non-GAAP measure is not indicative of overall Company performance and profitability because this measure does not directly accrue benefit to the shareholders due to the nature of costs excluded.
We define Restaurant operating margin as Company sales less Food and beverage costs, Restaurant labor and Restaurant expenses. We believe this metric provides a more useful comparison between periods and enables investors to focus on the performance of restaurant-level operations by excluding revenues not related to Company-owned restaurants, corporate General and administrative expenses, Depreciation and amortization, and Other (gains) and charges. Restaurant operating margin as presented may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other companies in our industry.
