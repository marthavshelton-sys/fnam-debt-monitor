Mexico City, June 8th, | 2026
Citigroup initiates coverage of Quálitas Controladora
Quálitas Controladora, S.A.B. de C.V. (“Quálitas", "the Company" or "the Group") (BMV: Q*),
informs its shareholders and investment community that Citigroup has initiated coverage of Q* as
of June 7th, 2026.
The fundamentals that determine its recommendation are: “Premium Insurer at a Premium
Valuation — Quálitas benefits from a strong brand recognition and a vertically integrated model
that supports superior combined ratios, higher underwriting profitability, and consistently elevated
ROEs (>20%).”

About Quálitas
Quálitas Controladora (Q) is the largest auto insurance company in Mexico in terms of market
share, with operations in El Salvador, Costa Rica, USA, Peru and Colombia. A unique business
model and more than 32 years of experience in the auto insurance segment has allowed the
Company to provide top quality service under the largest network in Mexico. Quálitas is listed on
the Mexican Stock Exchange (BMV) under the ticker “Q” (Bloomberg: Q*:MM).
