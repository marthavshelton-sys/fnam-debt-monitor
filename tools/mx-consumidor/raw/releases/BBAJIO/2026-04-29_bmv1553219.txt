Chief Executive Officer Resume
Iván Lomelí León
▪ Assume the role of Chief Executive Officer of BanBajío, on May 1, 2026.
▪ Mr. Lomelí has worked at BanBajío since May 2022 as Executive Director of
Corporate Banking for the Mexico City metropolitan area. Since January
has extended his responsibilities to include the northern and southern
regions of Mexico under his executive management.
▪ With thirty-five years of experience in the banking sector, most of it in
Scotiabank, where he ascended to the position of Deputy Chief Executive
Officer of Corporate and Global Transaction Banking.
▪ Mr. Lomelí holds a Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration,
specializing in Banking and Financial Services, from the Instituto
Tecnológico y de Estudios Superiores de Monterrey (ITESM), and a Master
of Business Administration from the European University in Amberes,
Belgium.
