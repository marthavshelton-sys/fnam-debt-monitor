Passengers decreased 7.1%, reaching an 84.1% load factor 
 
Mexico City, May 12, 202 6 – Viva announced today its April 2026 preliminary traffic 
results. Viva’s passenger traffic contracted 7.1% compared to April 2025, reaching 2.3 
million passengers. 
Total capacity in available seat miles (ASMs) decreased 7.8% compared to April 2025, 
reflecting a 4.6% reduction in domestic capacity and a 20.8% decrease in international 
capacity. Network load factor closed at 84.1%, with domestic and international load 
factors of 85.7% and 76.3%, respectively. 
“Passenger volumes decreased by 7.1% during April, with a load factor of 84.1%. These 
results reflect proactive capacity adjustments to align with the current macroeconomic 
environment and elevated fuel prices . In this context, we remain committed to a 
disciplined operational approach, prioritizing reliability as we manage ongoing Pratt & 
Whitney engine groundings and closely monitor market conditions  amid a cautious 
consumer backdrop”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers 
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor:  Represents the number of miles flown by scheduled passengers (RPMs) divided by 
scheduled available seat miles (ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva 
Viva is a Mexican ultra-low-cost airline that provides air transport services across Mexico, United States, 
and other Latin American countries. Founded in 2006, Viva operates one of the youngest aircraft fleets 
in America. With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva is focused on democratizing 
air travel by offering low fares and a low cost service. For more information please visit: 
https://www.vivaaerobus.com/en-us/
