Passengers increased 11.3%, reaching an 88.0% Load Factor  
 
Mexico City, July 9, 2024 – Viva Aerobus announced today its June 2024 preliminary 
traffic results. Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 11.3% compared to June 
2023, reaching 2.3 million passengers, driven by an increase of 11.5% and 9.7% in 
domestic and international  traffic, respectively. Load factors  reached 88.0% 
compared to 87.1% in 2023, with a load factor mix of 89.6% and 80.8% in domestic 
and international, respectively. 
Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity  in available seat miles (ASMs) by 9.9% 
compared to June 2023, reflecting an increase of 9.4% and 11.9% in domestic and 
international, respectively. 
“June saw a positive increase in traffic, outpacing our  capacity growth and 
enhancing our load factor, driven by robust demand across our markets. As we 
prepare for the upcoming peak summer season , booking trends remain strong, 
reflecting a solid demand environment. However, we must also navigate the 
persistent operational challenges stemming from the P&W engine recalls  and 
extreme weather events”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva Aerobus.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents  the number of seats available for passengers  
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor: Represents the number of miles flown by passenger (RPMs) divided by available seat miles 
(ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva Aerobus 
Viva Aerobus is Mexico’s ultra -low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of June 2024 it 
operates one of the youngest  aircraft fleets in  America. With a clear vision to give all people the 
opportunity to fly, Viva Aerobus has democratized the airline industry with one of the lowest fares in 
Mexico and one of the lowest cost structures in America, making their flights the best value offer. For 
more information please visit: https://www.vivaaerobus.com/en-us/
