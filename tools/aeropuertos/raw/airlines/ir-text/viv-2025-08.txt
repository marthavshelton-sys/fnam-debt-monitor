Passengers increased 8.2%, reaching an 89.5% load factor 
  
Mexico City, September 12, 2025 – Viva announced today its August 2025 preliminary 
traffic results. Viva ’s passenger traffic increased  8.2% compared to August 2024, 
reaching 2.7 million passengers, driven by an increase of 8.0% and 9.9% in domestic and 
international traffic, respectively.  
Viva grew its total capacity in available seat miles (ASMs) by 4.9% compared to August 
2024, reflecting an increase of 3.8% and 9.8% in domestic and international, 
respectively. Load factors improved to 89.5% from 89.2% in 2024, with a load factor mix 
of 90.9% and 83.0% in domestic and international, respectively. 
“Passenger volumes rose 8.2% in August, driven by balanced growth in domestic and 
international markets as the summer season concluded. Load factors improved as our 
network demonstrated resilience and benefitted from the extended summer school 
break. Capacity deployment remains disciplined as we adjust to the current demand 
conditions. While the Pratt & Whitney engine situation has drawn less attention over 
time, it continues to affect our operations and we remain focused on mitigating its 
impact.”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers 
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor:  Represents the number of miles flown by scheduled passengers (RPMs) divided by 
scheduled available seat miles (ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva 
Viva is Mexico’s ultra -low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of  August 2025 it operates 
one of the youngest aircraft fleets in America. With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to
