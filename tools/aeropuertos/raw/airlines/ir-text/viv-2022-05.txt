 
PRESS RELEASE 
 
 
VIVA AEROBUS REPORTS  
MONTHLY PASSENGER TRAFFIC MAY 2022 
1.75 M Pax; +29.7% vs May 2021 / LF: 89.3% 
 
Mexico City, June 03, 2022. – Viva Aerobus announced today its May 2022 
preliminary traffic results. This month, Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 
29.7% vs May 2021, totaling 1.75 million passengers, driven by an increase of 
38.7% in domestic traffic. Load factors reached 89.3%; an increase of 0.9 pp vs 
May 2021, reflecting a 92.4% and 77.4% load factor in domestic and international, 
respectively.   
 
As a result, Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity, measured in available seat miles 
(ASMs), by 33.7% vs May 2021. Domestically, Viva grew its capacity by 33.6% and 
by 34.2%, internationally. 
Year-to-date (January -May), Viva ’s traffic exceeded  7.68 million passengers, a 
51.4% increase vs 2021, with load factors increasing 3.2 pp to 84.9%.  
“Demand remained strong during May with load factors near pre -pandemic levels 
at 89%, further building on the  positive momentum seen throughout the year  as 
we head into summer. These results reflect our ability to capture demand while 
preserving profitability, a strategic pillar of our ultra-low-cost business model. Also, 
it will be important to remain disciplined and adapt to the current changing 
demand,” said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva Aerobus.  
In Viva´s continued effort to strengthen its service and improve air connectivity in 
Mexico, the airline announced the opening of five new routes in Felipe Angeles 
International Airport, the resumption of its two nonstop services from Toluca to 
Cancun and Monterrey and added two new routes from this same destin ation to 
Merida and Puerto Vallarta.

Glossary 
- Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
- ASM (Available Seat Miles): the number of seats available for passengers multiplied by the number of miles the 
seats are flown. They are calculated by multiplying the available seats for a given aircraft by the number of miles 
that aircraft will travel for a given flight. 
- RPM (Revenue Passenger Miles): the number of miles flown by passengers.  
- Load Factor: RPMs divided by ASMs and expressed as a percentage.
