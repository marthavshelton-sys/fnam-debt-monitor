Passengers increased 13.3%, reaching an 87.1% load factor 
 
Mexico City, January 10, 2025 – Viva announced today its December 2024 preliminary 
traffic results. Viva ’s passenger traffic increased 13. 3% compared to December 2023, 
reaching 2. 6 million passengers, driven by an increase of 1 0.6% and 3 5.1% in domestic 
and international traffic, respectively. Load factors reached 87. 1% compared to 8 5.0% 
in 2023, with a load factor mix of 88. 5% and 8 1.6% in domestic and international, 
respectively. 
Viva grew  its total capacity in available seat miles (ASMs) by 7.5% compared to 
December 2023, reflecting an increase of 4.0% and 22.6% in domestic and international, 
respectively. 
“We closed the year  on a strong note, achieving the highest number of passengers in 
our history of 27 .7 million while maintaining healthy load factors . This accomplishment 
comes despite the challenges posed by the ongoing Pratt & Whitney engine recall . I’m 
very proud of our Team Viva resilience and discipline d management in navigating one 
of the most demanding operational environments in recent memory . As we look ahead 
to 2025, we remain focused on maintaining  our operational reliability and disciplined 
capacity management in an evolving environment”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of 
Viva Aerobus.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not.  
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers 
multiplied by the number of miles.  
Load Factor:  Represents the number of miles flown by scheduled passengers (RPMs) divided by 
scheduled available seat miles (ASMs) and expressed as a percentage.  
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers.
