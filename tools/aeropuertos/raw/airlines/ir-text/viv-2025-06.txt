Passengers increased 6.1%, reaching an 86.0% load factor 
  
Mexico City, July 11, 202 5 – Viva announced today its June 2025 preliminary traffic 
results. Viva’s passenger traffic increased 6.1% compared to June 2024, reaching 2. 5 
million passengers, driven by an increase of 5.8% and 9.2% in domestic and international 
traffic, respectively.  
Viva grew its total capacity in available seat miles (ASMs) by 6.8% compared to June 
2024, reflecting an increase of 6.1% and 9.8% in domestic and international, 
respectively. Load factors reached 86.0% compared to 88.7% in 2024, with a load factor 
mix of 87.1% and 80.9% in domestic and international, respectively. 
“June delivered solid traffic performance with passengers increasing 6.1% YoY and a 
load factor of 86.0%. As we move into the summer season, we remain focused on 
matching capacity to demand, reflecting a conservative stance as consumer behavior 
continues to adjust in a more cautious  environment. We keep working to maintain 
operational efficiency and reliability given the ongoing the Pratt & Whitney engine 
issues”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers 
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor:  Represents the number of miles flown by scheduled passengers (RPMs) divided by 
scheduled available seat miles (ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers.
