Passengers increased 17.6%, reaching an 86.6% load factor 
 
Mexico City, May 12, 202 5 – Viva announced today its April 2025 preliminary traffic 
results. Viva’s passenger traffic increased 17.6% compared to April 2024, reaching 2. 5 
million passengers, driven by an increase of 15.3% and 39.0% in domestic and 
international traffic, respectively. Load factors reached 86.6% compared to 83.9% in 
2024, with a load factor mix of 8 8.8% and 77.3% in domestic and international, 
respectively. 
Viva grew its total capacity in available seat miles (ASMs) by 10.4% compared to April 
2024, reflecting an increase of 6.4% and 29.8% in domestic and international, 
respectively. 
“April traffic increased 17.6% on a healthy load factor of 86.6%, a 2.8 pp improvement 
compared to the same period last year. This was mainly dri ven by the timing shift of 
Easter holidays to April this year and ongoing network optimization efforts. We continue 
to manage capacity cautiously amid a volatile macro backdrop and cautious 
consumer behavior primarily in off-peak periods. As the Pratt & Whitney engine issues 
continue to pressure the efficiency of our operations, we maintain as a priority to secure 
operational reliability.”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers 
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor:  Represents the number of miles flown by scheduled passengers (RPMs) divided by 
scheduled available seat miles (ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers.
