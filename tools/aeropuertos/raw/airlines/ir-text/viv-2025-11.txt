Passengers increased 4.8%, reaching an 85.2% load factor 
Mexico City, December 11, 202 5 – Viva announced today its November 2025 
preliminary traffic results. Viva ’s passenger traffic increased  4.8% compared to 
November 2024, reaching 2.6 million passengers. 
Viva grew  its total capacity in available seat miles (ASMs) by 4.9% compared to 
November 2024, reflecting an increase of 5.1% in domestic capacity and an increase 
of 3.7% in international. Load factors stood at 85.2% from 87.4% in 202 4, with a load 
factor of 86.8% and 78.0% in domestic and international, respectively. 
“During November, passenger volumes grew 4.8%, achieving an 85.2% load factor.  This 
performance was mainly driven by the continued resilience of our core domestic 
market, providing a strong volume base as we enter the peak holiday travel period.  
While the operational environment remains constrained by engine availability issues, we 
continue to prioritize operational stability and disciplined execution to ensure reliable 
service during the holiday season.”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers 
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor:  Represents the number of miles flown by scheduled passengers (RPMs) divided by 
scheduled available seat miles (ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva 
Viva is Mexico’s ultra-low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of November 2025 it operates 
one of the youngest aircraft fleets in America. With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to 
fly, Viva has democratized the airline industry with one of the lowest fares in Mexico and one of the
