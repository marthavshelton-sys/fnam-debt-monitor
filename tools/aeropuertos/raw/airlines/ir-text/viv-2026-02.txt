Passengers increased 1.9%, reaching an 84.6% load factor 
 
Mexico City, March 12, 202 6 – Viva announced today its February 2026 preliminary 
traffic results. Viva ’s passenger traffic increased  1.9% compared to February 2025, 
reaching 2.1 million passengers. 
Total capacity in available seat miles (ASMs) decreased 2.8% compared to February 
2025, reflecting a 1.3% reduction in domestic capacity and a 9.2% contraction in 
international capacity. Load factors increased by 1.6 percentage points standing at 
84.6%, with a load factor of 85.9% and 78.7% in domestic and international, respectively. 
“Passenger traffic rose 1.9% in February supported by a 3.1% increase in the domestic 
market. Despite typical first quarter seasonality and late month operational disruptions, 
our load factor reached 84.6%, reflecting effective capacity allocation  efforts. We 
maintain a flexible operational stance, proactively managing capacity  to adjust to 
market conditions while ensuring reliability amid the ongoing Pratt & Whitney engine 
groundings”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers 
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor:  Represents the number of miles flown by scheduled passengers (RPMs) divided by 
scheduled available seat miles (ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva 
Viva is a Mexican ultra-low-cost airline that provides air transport services across Mexico, United States, 
and other Latin American countries. Founded in 2006, Viva operates one of the youngest aircraft fleets 
in America. With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva is focused on democratizing 
air travel by offering low fares and a low cost service. For more information please visit:
