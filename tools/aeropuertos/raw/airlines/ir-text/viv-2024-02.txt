Passengers increased 22.3%, reaching an 85.2% Load Factor  
 
Mexico City, March 8 , 202 4 – Viva Aerobus announced today its February 2024 
preliminary traffic results . Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 22.3% 
compared to February 2023, reaching 1.9 million passengers, driven by an increase 
of 23.9% and 8.5% in domestic and international traffic, respectively. Load factors  
reached 85.2% compared to 83.6% in 2023, with a load factor mix of 88.0% and 72.8% 
in domestic and international, respectively. 
Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity  in available seat miles (ASMs) by 21.2% 
compared to February 2023, reflecting an increase of 22.4% and 15.8% in domestic 
and international, respectively. 
“As a result of the favorable market dynamics in the Mexican market, our load 
factors remained healthy at 85%, increasing 1.6pp in one of the slowest seasonal 
months in Mexico. After a positive performance in 2023, we have had a solid start to 
the year  despite continued challenges from the Pratt & Whitney GTF preventive 
engine inspections , reinforcing our  commitment to disciplined capacity 
deployement and ensuring operational reliability” said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of 
Viva Aerobus

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents  the number of seats available for passengers  
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor: Represents the number of miles flown by passenger (RPMs) divided by available seat miles 
(ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva Aerobus 
Viva Aerobus is Mexico’s ultra -low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of February 2024 it 
operates one of the youngest  aircraft fleets in  America. With a clear vision to give all people the 
opportunity to fly, Viva Aerobus has democratized the airline industry with one of the lowest fares in 
Mexico and one of the lowest cost structures in America, making their flights the best value offer. For
