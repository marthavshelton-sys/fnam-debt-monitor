 
VIVA AEROBUS REPORTS MONTHLY AND ANNUAL PASSENGER TRAFFIC 
DECEMBER: 1.6 M PAX; + 46% VS DEC. 19 / ANNUAL: 15.2 M PAX; +27% VS 2019 
Mexico City, January 6, 2022.- Monthly passenger traffic in December 2021 exceeded, for the first time, 
more than 1.6 million passengers a 46% growth vs December 2019 and with an 84% load factor. 
 
Full year 2021 Viva´s annual traffic increased 27% vs. 2019, with more than 15.2 million passengers with an 
84% load factor. 
 
“Our ultra-low cost business model allowed us to continue stimulating demand and to serve more than 15 
million passengers last year, exceeding our pre-pandemic levels with a healthy load factor despite the Covid-
19 waves throughout the year. Our non -stop service, low fares, new fleet and unmatched reliability have 
been crucial for our customers preference and confidence in air travel during this pandemic”, said Juan 
Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva Aerobus..

Glossary 
• Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the 
report, regardless of whether they flew or not. 
• ASM (Available Seat Kilometers): the number of seats available for passengers multiplied by the 
number of kilometers the seats are flown. They are calculated by multiplying the available seats for 
a given aircraft by the number of kilometers that aircraft will travel for a given flight. 
• RPM (Revenue Passenger Kilometers): the number of kilometers flown by passengers. 
• Load Factor: RPKs divided by ASKs and expressed as a percentage.
