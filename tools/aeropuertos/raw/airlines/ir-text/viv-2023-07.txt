Passengers increased 22.6%, reaching an 90.5% Load Factor  
 
Mexico City, August 7, 202 3 – Viva Aerobus announced today its July 2023 
preliminary traffic results . Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 22.6% 
compared to July 2022, reaching 2.4 million passengers, driven by an increase of 
23.1% and 18.7% in domestic and international traffic, respectively . Load factors  
reached 90.5% compared to 89.5% in 2022, with a load factor mix of 91.4% and 86.1% 
in domestic and international, respectively. 
Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity  in available seat miles (ASMs) by 17.5% 
compared to July 2022, reflecting an increase of 18.1% and 14.8% in domestic and 
international, respectively. 
“July traffic reflects the strength of the summer season in our markets, in line with the 
solid demand we have seen throughout 2023 . We delivered our highest monthly 
mark of 2. 4 million passengers  while maintaining a disciplin ed approach toward 
capacity, as reflected by our strong load factor of 90.5% . We continue to closely 
monitor the challenging operational environment to serve the robust demand.” said 
Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva Aerobus.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents  the number of seats available for passengers  
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor: Represents the number of miles flown by passenger (RPMs) divided by available seat miles 
(ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva Aerobus 
Viva Aerobus is Mexico’s ultra -low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of July 2023 it 
operates one of the youngest Latin American fleets with 75 Airbus: 44 Airbus A320 and 31 Airbus A321. 
With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva Aerobus has democratized the  airline 
industry with one of the lowest fares in Mexico and one of the lowest cost structures in America, making 
their flights the best value offer. For more information please visit: http://www.vivaaerobus.com/en
