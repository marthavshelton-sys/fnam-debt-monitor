 
PRESS RELEASE 
 
 
VIVA AEROBUS REPORTS  
MONTHLY PASSENGER TRAFFIC APRIL 2022 
1.68 M Pax; +44.9% vs April 21 / LF: 84.6% 
 
 
Mexico City, May 05, 2022. – Viva Aerobus announced today its April 2022 
preliminary traffic results. This month, Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 
44.9% vs April 2021, totaling 1.68 million passengers. Load factor reached 84.6%; 
an increase of 2.2 pp vs April 2021.   
 
Viva´s domestic traffic exceeded 1.49 million passengers, a 49.8% increase 
compared to April 2021, with an 87.9% load factor. International traffic totaled 180 
thousand passengers, a 14.1% growth vs April 2021, with a 71.2% load factor. 
 
As a result, Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity, measured in available seat miles 
(ASMs), by 46.6% vs April 2021. Domestically, Viva grew its capacity by 39.2% and 
by 86.7%, internationally. 
Year-to-date (January -April), Viva ’s traffic exceeded  5.93 million p assengers, a 
59.2% increase vs 2021, with load factors increasing 4.2 pp to 83.6%.  
“We continued to see positive demand trends during April, driven by leisure, VFR, 
and SME´s business passengers, reflecting the strength of our ultra low -cost 
business model. As we head into summer, we are focused on maintaining our 
disciplined approach towards capacity, while preserving profitability.”, said Juan 
Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva Aerobus. “Also, we started the Medellin -Mexico City 
and Nuevo Laredo-Mexico City flights, while adding a new Airbus A321, ending the 
month with 58 aircraft”, he added.

Glossary 
- Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
- ASM (Available Seat Miles): the number of seats available for passengers multiplied by the number of miles the 
seats are flown. They are calculated by multiplying the available seats for a given aircraft by the number of miles 
that aircraft will travel for a given flight. 
- RPM (Revenue Passenger Miles): the number of miles flown by passengers.  
- Load Factor: RPMs divided by ASMs and expressed as a percentage.
