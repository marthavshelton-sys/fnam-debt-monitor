Passengers increased 25.4%, reaching an 88.1% Load Factor  
 
Mexico City, September 8, 2023 – Viva Aerobus announced today its August 2023 
preliminary traffic results . Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 25.4% 
compared to August 2022, reaching 2.4 million passengers, driven by an increase of 
25.3% and 26.2% in domestic and international traffic, respectively . Load factors  
reached 88.1% compared to 85.7% in 2022, with a load factor mix of 89.0% and 83.5% 
in domestic and international, respectively. 
Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity  in available seat miles (ASMs) by 18.2% 
compared to August 2022, reflecting an increase of 18.3% and 17.9% in domestic 
and international, respectively. 
“The summer season ended strong . August traffic reached 2.4 million passengers , 
marking the fifth consecutive month with over 2 million passengers,  on a high load 
factor of 8 8.1%. Our domestic market  load factor  remained robust at 89.0%, 
reflecting our disciplined capacity deployment. Our unique and well-positioned 
network continues to support our profitable growth in the Mexican market, and we 
will remain highly focused as we enter a slower seasonal period in September.” said 
Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva Aerobus

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents  the number of seats available for passengers  
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor: Represents the number of miles flown by passenger (RPMs) divided by available seat miles 
(ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva Aerobus 
Viva Aerobus is Mexico’s ultra -low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of August 2023 it 
operates one of the youngest Latin American fleets with 77 Airbus: 45 Airbus A320 and 32 Airbus A321. 
With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva Aerobus has democratized the airline 
industry with one of the lowest fares in Mexico and one of the lowest cost structures in America, making 
their flights the best value offer. For more information please visit: http://www.vivaaerobus.com/en
