 
PRESS RELEASE 
 
 
VIVA AEROBUS REPORTS  
MONTHLY PASSENGER TRAFFIC JUNE 2022 
1.75 M Pax; +28.5% vs June 2021 / LF: 90.6% 
 
Mexico City, July 06, 2022. – Viva Aerobus announced today its June 2022 
preliminary traffic results. This month, Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 
28.5% vs June 2021, totaling 1.75 million passengers, driven by an increase of 
34.2% in domestic traffic. Load factors reached 90.6%; an increase of 2.5 pp vs 
June 2021, reflecting a 92.7% and 81.9% load factor in domestic and international, 
respectively.   
 
As a result, Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity, measured in available seat miles 
(ASMs), by 28.4% vs June 2021. Domestically, Viva grew its capacity by 28.8% and 
by 26.9%, internationally. 
Year-to-date (January -June), Viva ’s traffic exceeded  9.43 million passengers, a 
46.5% increase vs 2021, with load factors increasing 2.9 pp to 85.9%.  
“June traffic remained strong, reflecting a robust demand in our core leisure and 
VFR markets, as well as SME’s business travelers . As a result, our load factor 
reached 90.6%, the highest level since 2019. However, we continue to navigate  
through the short -term challenges , given the supply chain disruptions in a high 
demand environment that has impacted the overall industry. At the same time, we 
are closely monitoring demand trends as we enter peak summer travel, focusing on 
providing a good customer experience .” said  Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva 
Aerobus.

Glossary 
- Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
- ASM (Available Seat Miles): the number of seats available for passengers multiplied by the number of miles the 
seats are flown. They are calculated by multiplying the available seats for a given aircraft by the number of miles 
that aircraft will travel for a given flight. 
- RPM (Revenue Passenger Miles): the number of miles flown by passengers.  
- Load Factor: RPMs divided by ASMs and expressed as a percentage.
