Passengers decreased 1.4%, reaching an 89.0% load factor 
 
Mexico City, June 8, 202 6 – Viva announced today its May 2026 preliminary traffic 
results. Viva’s passenger traffic decreased 1.4% compared to May 2025, reaching 2. 5 
million passengers. 
Total capacity in available seat miles (ASMs) decreased 11.1% compared to May 2025, 
reflecting a 7.7% reduction in domestic capacity and a 26.3% decrease in international 
capacity. Network load factor increased from 84.8% to 89.0%, with domestic and 
international load factors of 90.2% and 82.2%, respectively. 
“May traffic reflected the proactive capacity adjustments we have implemented in 
response to the current macroeconomic environment and elevated fuel prices . 
Passenger volumes decreased 1.4%, while the load factor improved to 89.0%, supported 
by disciplined capacity deployment, particularly across international markets . While 
Pratt & Whitney engine groundings continue to constrain fleet availability and the 
consumer environment remains cautious, we continue prioritizing operational reliability 
and aligning capacity with prevailing demand conditions”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, 
CEO of Viva.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers 
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor:  Represents the number of miles flown by scheduled passengers (RPMs) divided by 
scheduled available seat miles (ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva 
Viva is a Mexican ultra-low-cost airline that provides air transport services across Mexico, United States, 
and other Latin American countries. Founded in 2006, Viva operates one of the youngest aircraft fleets 
in America. With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva is focused on democratizing 
air travel by offering low fares and a low cost service. For more information please visit: 
https://www.vivaaerobus.com/en-us/
