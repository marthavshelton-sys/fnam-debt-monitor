Passengers increased 4.3%, reaching an 82.8% load factor 
Mexico City, January 12, 2026 – Viva announced today its December 2025 preliminary 
traffic results. Viva ’s passenger traffic increased  4.3% compared to December 2024, 
reaching 2.7 million passengers. 
Viva grew  its total capacity in available seat miles (ASMs) by 5.8% compared to 
December 2024, reflecting a 5.2% increase in domestic capacity and an 8.0% increase 
in international capacity. Load factors stood at 82.8% compared to 87.1% in 2024, with 
a load factor of 84.9% and 75.4% in domestic and international, respectively. 
“December passenger volumes increased 4.3%, driven by seasonal demand. Our load 
factor of 82.8% reflected weather disruptions in Tijuana and international capacity 
reallocation from the new Mexico City Airport (AIFA) . Throughout the year, we 
managed a complex industry environment with discipline and resilience, overcoming 
pressures from Pratt & Whitney engine recalls and a challenging macro backdrop.  
Despite these challenges, w e concluded the year reaching the highest passenger 
figure in our history with close to 30 million.” said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers 
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor:  Represents the number of miles flown by scheduled passengers (RPMs) divided by 
scheduled available seat miles (ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva 
Viva is a Mexican ultra-low-cost airline that provides air transport services across Mexico, United States, 
and other Latin American countries. Founded in 2006, Viva operates one of the youngest aircraft fleets 
in America. With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva is focused on democratizing 
air travel by offering low fares and a low cost service. For more information please visit: 
https://www.vivaaerobus.com/en-us/
