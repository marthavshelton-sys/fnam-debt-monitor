Mexico City, September 11, 2026 – Viva announced today its August 2026 preliminary 
traffic results. Viva transported 2.7 million passengers in August 2026, a decrease of 1.2% 
compared to August 2025.  
Total capacity in available seat miles (ASMs) decreased 3.5% compared to August 2025, 
reflecting a reduction of 4.0% in domestic capacity and 1.0% in international capacity. 
Network load factor increased to 89.8%, with domestic and international load factors 
of 90.9% and 84.7%, respectively. 
“During August, we continued to proactively align our network with evolving demand 
conditions, consistent with the disciplined approach we have maintained throughout 
the year. Passenger volumes decreased 1.2%, while our load factor improved to 89.8%, 
supported by the effectiveness of our network adjustments . With fuel costs remaining 
elevated and Pratt & Whitney engine groundings continuing to constrain fleet 
availability, we will remain focused on the factors within our control, including 
disciplined capacity management  and operational reliability, while preserving 
liquidity”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers 
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor:  Represents the number of miles flown by scheduled passengers (RPMs) divided by 
scheduled available seat miles (ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva 
Viva is a Mexican ultra-low-cost airline that provides air transport services across Mexico, United States, 
and other Latin American countries. Founded in 2006, Viva operates one of the youngest aircraft fleets 
in America. With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva is focused on democratizing 
air travel by offering low fares and a low -cost service. For more information please visit: 
https://www.vivaaerobus.com/en-us/
