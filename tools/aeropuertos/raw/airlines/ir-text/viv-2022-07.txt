Passengers increased 22.8%, reaching an 89.5% Load Factor  
 
Mexico City, August 5, 202 2 – Viva Aerobus announced today its July 2022 
preliminary traffic results . This month, Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 
22.8% to 1.95 million passengers compared to July 2021, mainly driven by an increase 
of 26.3% in domestic traffic. Load factors  increased 1.5 p.p. to 89.5%, reflecting a 
90.1% and 86.8% load factor in domestic and international, respectively.   
As a result, Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity, measured in available seat miles 
(ASMs), by 2 5.0% compared to July 2021. Domestically, Viva grew its capacity by 
25.7% and by 21.9%, internationally. 
Year-to-date (January-July), Viva’s traffic reached 11.38 million passengers, a 41.8% 
increase compared to 2021, with load factors increasing 2.6 p.p. to 86.5%.  
“Strong demand continued with the much-anticipated summer season which now 
started later in the month of July compared to previous years and will extend until 
late August . Our load factors recovered to 89.5%, reflecting our commitment 
towards a disciplined growth . We remain cautious and closely monitoring  the 
current macroeconomic and supply chain environment, managing capacity and 
on providing a good customer experience” , said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva 
Aerobus.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers  
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor: Represents the number of miles flown by passenger (RPMs) divided by available seat miles 
(ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva Aerobus 
Viva Aerobus is Mexico’s ultra -low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and today it operates the 
youngest Latin American fleet with 62 Airbus: 40 Airbus A320 and 22 Airbus A321. With a clear vision to 
give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva Aerobus has democratized the airline industry with the lowest 
fares in Mexico and the lowest cost structure in America, making their flights the  best value offer. For
