Passengers increased 25.5%, reaching an 83.6% Load Factor  
 
Mexico City, March 8, 202 3 – Viva Aerobus announced today its February 2023 
preliminary traffic results . Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 25.5% to 1.53 
million passengers compared to February 2022, driven by an increase of 26.2% and 
20.4% in domestic and international traffic, respectively. Load factors reached 83.6% 
compared to 84.3% in 2022, reflecting an 85.3% and 76.5% load factor in domestic 
and international, respectively. 
Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity  in available seat miles (ASMs) by 25.5% 
compared to February 2022, reflecting an increase of 26.1% and 22.7% in domestic 
and international, respectively. 
“February traffic had a solid growth compared to 202 2, reflecting the positive 
demand in both domestic and international markets. Given the robust environment, 
we continue our  expansion efforts  in the international market through our newly 
announced Monterrey-Bogota route , increasing our growth in the third largest 
international air traffic market for Mexico,” said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva 
Aerobus.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents  the number of seats available for passengers  
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor: Represents the number of miles flown by passenger (RPMs) divided by available seat miles 
(ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva Aerobus 
Viva Aerobus is Mexico’s ultra -low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of February 2023 it 
operates one of the youngest Latin American fleets with 69 Airbus: 43 Airbus A320 and 26 Airbus A321. 
With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva Aerobus has democratized the airline 
industry with one of the lowest fares in Mexico and one of the lowest cost structure in America, making 
their flights the best value offer. For more information please visit: http://www.vivaaerobus.com/en
