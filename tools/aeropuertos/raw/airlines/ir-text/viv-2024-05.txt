Passengers increased 13.0%, reaching an 84.5% Load Factor  
 
Mexico City, June 10 , 202 4 – Viva Aerobus announced today its May 2024 
preliminary traffic results . Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 13.0% 
compared to May 2023, reaching 2.3 million passengers, driven by an increase of 
13.5% and 7.6% in domestic  and international  traffic, respectively. Load factors  
reached 84.5% compared to 86.8% in 2023, with a load factor mix of 86.2% and 75.6% 
in domestic and international, respectively. 
Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity  in available seat miles (ASMs) by 16.4% 
compared to May 2023, reflecting an increase of 17.5% and 11.1% in domestic and 
international, respectively. 
“In May, we observed a sequential recovery in our load factors of 1.2pp, reflecting 
good passenger demand in a shoulder month . Current booking trends indicate a 
positive summer season across our markets . I would like to recognize our teams , 
which are doing a great job  navigating the persistent operati onal challenges 
stemming from the P&W engine  recalls”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua , CEO of Viva 
Aerobus.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents  the number of seats available for passengers  
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor: Represents the number of miles flown by passenger (RPMs) divided by available seat miles 
(ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva Aerobus 
Viva Aerobus is Mexico’s ultra -low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of May 2024 it 
operates one of the youngest  aircraft fleets in  America. With a clear vision to give all people the 
opportunity to fly, Viva Aerobus has democratized the airline industry with one of the lowest fares in 
Mexico and one of the lowest cost structures in America, making their flights the best value offer. For
