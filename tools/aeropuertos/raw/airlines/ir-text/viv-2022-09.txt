Passengers increased 32.6%, reaching an 84.1% Load Factor  
 
Mexico City, October 5, 2022 – Viva Aerobus announced today its September 2022 
preliminary traffic results . Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 32.6% to 1.64 
million passengers compared to September 2021, driven by an increase of 37.2% 
and 2.4% in domestic and international traffic, respectively. Load factors increased 
4.5 p.p. to 84.1% compared to 2021, reflecting an 84.9% and 80.3% load factor in 
domestic and international, respectively. 
Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity  in available seat miles (ASMs) by 25.3% 
compared to September 2021, reflecting an increase of 29.4% and 8.9% in domestic 
and international, respectively. 
Year-to-date (January-September), Viva’s traffic reached 14.90 million passengers, 
a 39.9% increase compared to 2021, with load factors increasing 3.5 p.p. to 86.1%.  
“Demand continued to grow in September, with passenger traffic increasing 32.6% 
and load factors growing 4.5 p.p. compared to 2021, on a higher capacity of 25.3%, 
reflecting our balanced approach towards growth. As part of our focus on growing 
our business, we are excited about our n ew loyalty program  Doters 
(www.doters.com), and our newly launched co-brand card HSBC Viva. Doters and 
HSBC Viva will be essential in building and strengthening our brand and relationship 
with our customers and will ultimately improve the travel experience.”, said Juan 
Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva Aerobus.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents  the number of seats available for passengers  
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor: Represents the number of miles flown by passenger (RPMs) divided by available seat miles 
(ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva Aerobus 
Viva Aerobus is Mexico’s ultra-low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of September 2022 
it operates one of the youngest Latin American fleets with 65 Airbus: 42 Airbus A320 and 23 Airbus A321. 
With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva Aerobus has democratized the airline 
industry with the lowest fares in Mexico and the lowest cost structure in America, making their flights 
the best value offer. For more information please visit: http://www.vivaaerobus.com/en
