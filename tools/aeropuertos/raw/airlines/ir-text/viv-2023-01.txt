Passengers increased 20.2%, reaching an 83.2% Load Factor  
 
Mexico City, February 8, 2023 – Viva Aerobus announced today its January 2023 
preliminary traffic results . Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 20.2% to 1.75 
million passengers compared to January 2022, driven by an increase of 22.4% and 
4.9% in domestic and international traffic, respectively. Load factors reached 83.2% 
compared to 79.1% in 2022, reflecting an 84.3% and 78.7% load factor in domestic 
and international, respectively. 
Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity  in available seat miles (ASMs) by 12.8% 
compared to January 2022, reflecting an increase of 15.6% and 2.2% in domestic 
and international, respectively. 
“In January, we saw strong demand in all our markets  with robust market 
fundamentals as demand outgrew capacity  growth. Despite operating in one of 
the lowest seasonal months in Mexico, we were capable of achieving healthy load 
factors. Based on forward booking trends, demand remains strong, marking a great 
start to the year,” said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva Aerobus.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents  the number of seats available for passengers  
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor: Represents the number of miles flown by passenger (RPMs) divided by available seat miles 
(ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva Aerobus 
Viva Aerobus is Mexico’s ultra -low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of January 2023 it 
operates one of the youngest Latin American fleets with 69 Airbus: 43 Airbus A320 and 26 Airbus A321. 
With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva Aerobus has democratized the airline 
industry with one of the lowest fares in Mexico and one of the lowest cost structure in America, making 
their flights the best value offer. For more information please visit: http://www.vivaaerobus.com/en
