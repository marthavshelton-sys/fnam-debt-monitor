 
PRESS RELEASE 
 
 
VIVA AEROBUS REPORTS  
MONTHLY PASSENGER TRAFFIC MARCH 2022 
1.57 M Pax; + 45.5% vs March 21 / LF: 86.8% 
 
 
Mexico City, April 07, 2022. – Viva Aerobus announced today its March 2022 preliminary 
traffic results. This month, Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 45.5% vs March 
2021, totaling 1.57 million passengers. Load factor reached 86.8%; an increase of 2pp 
from March 2021.   
 
Viva´s domestic traffic exceeded 1.38 million passengers, a 39.7% increase as compared 
to March 2021, with an 89.5% load factor. International traffic exceeded 190 thousand 
passengers, a 109% growth vs March 2021, with a 75.6% load factor. 
 
As a result, Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity, measured in available seat miles (ASMs), 
by 48% vs March 2021. Domestically, Viva grew its capacity by 31.5% and by 200% 
internationally. 
 
In the first quarter, Viva ´s traffic exceeded 4.25 million passengers, a 65.7% increase vs 
1Q21, with load factors up 5.1pp to 83.3% while growing capacity 61% (ASMs).  
 
“During March, we continued to see s olid demand trends in both domestic and 
international markets  post Omicron variant , however we remain committed  on 
maintaining a disciplined approach towards capacity , as reflected by our recovering load 
factors. Going forward, we should continue to match capacity to growing demand, while 
seeking opportunities to continue to grow in our markets. In April, we will start operations 
of our new flights to Medellin from Mexico City and Cancun ”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, 
CEO of Viva Aerobus.

Glossary 
- Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
- ASM (Available Seat Miles): the number of seats available for passengers multiplied by the number of miles the 
seats are flown. They are calculated by multiplying the available seats for a given aircraft by the number of miles 
that aircraft will travel for a given flight. 
- RPM (Revenue Passenger Miles): the number of miles flown by passengers.  
- Load Factor: RPMs divided by ASMs and expressed as a percentage.
