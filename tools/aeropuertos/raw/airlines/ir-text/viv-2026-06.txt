Passengers decreased 2.4%, reaching an 84.6% load factor 
 
Mexico City, July 10, 202 6 – Viva announced today its June 2026 preliminary traffic 
results. Viva’s passenger traffic declined 2.4% compared to June 2025, transporting 2.4 
million passengers. 
Total capacity in available seat miles (ASMs) decreased 4.1% compared to June 2025, 
reflecting a 4.8% reduction in domestic capacity and a 1.2% decrease in international 
capacity. Network load factor reached 84.6%, with domestic and international load 
factors of 85.9% and 78.6%, respectively. 
“During June, we maintained a disciplined approach to capacity deployment amid a 
softer macroeconomic environment and elevated fuel prices, while demand patterns 
were also affected by travel trends associated with the FIFA World Cup.  In line with 
broader industry trends, passenger volumes declined 2.4%, while our load factor stood 
at 84.6%. Looking ahead, we will continue to prioritize operational reliability and adjust 
our network to prevailing demand conditions, while ongoing Pratt & Whitney engine 
groundings continue to constrain fleet availability ”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of 
Viva.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers 
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor:  Represents the number of miles flown by scheduled passengers (RPMs) divided by 
scheduled available seat miles (ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva 
Viva is a Mexican ultra-low-cost airline that provides air transport services across Mexico, United States, 
and other Latin American countries. Founded in 2006, Viva operates one of the youngest aircraft fleets 
in America. With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva is focused on democratizing 
air travel by offering low fares and a low -cost service. For more information please visit:
