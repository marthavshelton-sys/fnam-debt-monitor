Passengers increased 27.6%, reaching an 85.0% Load Factor  
 
Mexico City, November 4, 2022 – Viva Aerobus announced today its October 2022 
preliminary traffic results . Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 27.6% to 1.85 
million passengers  compared to October 2021, driven by an increase of 31.8% 
domestic traffic. Load factors reached 85.0% compared to 85.5% in 2021, reflecting 
an 85.5% and 82.5% load factor in domestic and international, respectively. 
Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity  in available seat miles (ASMs) by 29.3% 
compared to October 2021, reflecting an increase of 33.3% and 12.5% in domestic 
and international, respectively. 
Year-to-date (January-October), Viva’s traffic reached 16.74 million passengers, a 
38.4% increase compared to 2021, with load factors increasing 3.0 p.p. to 86.0%.  
“In October, w e continue d to see a strong demand environment with robust 
booking trends for year -end, allowing us t o match capacity with demand . We 
expect these trends to favor a good yield environment in our markets. Our ultra low-
cost business model continues to  drive our decisions , focusing on a disciplined 
approach toward capacity and on growing our ancillary revenue. We continue to 
expand our network and strengthen our presence in the Mexico City metropolitan 
area, starting three new non-stop services from Toluca airport to Merida, Monterrey, 
and Cancun . Additionally, the Toluca-Puerto Vallarta  non-stop service will start 
operations in November and the New Mexico City Airport (AIFA)-Tijuana route in 
December.”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva Aerobus

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents  the number of seats available for passengers  
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor: Represents the number of miles flown by passenger (RPMs) divided by available seat miles 
(ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva Aerobus 
Viva Aerobus is Mexico’s ultra -low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of October 2022 it 
operates one of the youngest Latin American fleets with 66 Airbus: 42 Airbus A320 and 24 Airbus A321. 
With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva Aerobus has democratized the airline 
industry with the lowest fares in Mexico and the lowest cost structure in America, making their flights 
the best value offer. For more information please visit: http://www.vivaaerobus.com/en
