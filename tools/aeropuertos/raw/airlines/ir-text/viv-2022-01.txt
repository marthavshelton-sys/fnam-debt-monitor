 
VIVA AEROBUS REPORTS MONTHLY PASSENGER TRAFFIC: 1.45 M Pax; + 73.5% 
vs Jan. 21 / LF: 79.1% 
Mexico City, February 4, 2022.- Viva Aerobus announced today its January 2022 preliminary traffic results. 
This month, Viva Aerobus traffic increased 73.5% vs January 2021, totaling 1.45 million passengers. Load 
factor reached 79.1%; an increase of 6.8 points from January 2021. 
 
Viva´s domestic traffic was 1.27 million passengers, a 67% increase as compared to January 2021, with an 
81.1% load factor. International traffic was 185 thousand passengers, a 136.7% growth vs January 2021, with 
a 71.4% load factor. 
 
In January 2022, passenger d emand, measured in Revenue Passenger Miles (RPMs) in the domestic and 
international markets increased 63.4% and 181.7%, respectively, as compared 
to January 2021. Total demand (RPMs) was up 77.2% YoY. 
 
As a result, Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity, mea sured in available seat miles (ASMs), by 61.9% vs 
January 2021. Domestically, Viva grew its capacity by 49.3% and by 140.6% internationally. 
 
“Amid this ever -changing environment, we continue to grow our business, due to our low fares, non -stop 
flights, new aircraft, and unmatched reliability. We kicked off 2022 with network expansion, with three new 
nonstop services: Mexico City – Nuevo Laredo, Mexico City – Medellin and Cancun – Medellin,” said Juan 
Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva Aerobus..

Glossary 
• Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the 
report, regardless of whether they flew or not. 
• ASM (Available Seat Miles): the number of seats available for passengers multiplied by the 
number of miles the seats are flown. They are calculated by multiplying the available seats for a 
given aircraft by the number of miles that aircraft will travel for a given flight. 
• RPM (Revenue Passenger Miles): the number of miles flown by passengers. 
• Load Factor: RPMs divided by ASMs and expressed as a percentage.
