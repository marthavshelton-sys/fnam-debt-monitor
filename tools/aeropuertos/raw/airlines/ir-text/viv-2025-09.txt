Passengers increased 3.4%, reaching an 86.1% load factor 
Mexico City, October 10, 2025 – Viva announced today its September 2025 preliminary 
traffic results. Viva ’s passenger traffic increased  3.4% compared to September 2024, 
reaching 2.4 million passengers, driven by an increase of 3.6% and 2.0% in domestic and 
international traffic, respectively.  
Viva grew  its total capacity in available seat miles (ASMs) by 0.7% compared to 
September 2024, reflecting flat domestic capacity and an increase of 4.3% in 
international. Load factors stood at 86.1% from 88.1% in 2024, with a load factor mix of 
87.9% and 78.1% in domestic and international, respectively. 
“During September, p assenger volumes rose 3.4%, reflecting a continued balance 
between domestic and international markets  with a stable load factor of 8 6.1%. We 
continue adapting our network to current macroeconomic conditions, particularly 
during off -peak periods.  In addition to a complex demand environment , engine 
availability issues continue to pressure our operations, and we continue prioritizing efforts 
to mitigate their effects.”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers 
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor:  Represents the number of miles flown by scheduled passengers (RPMs) divided by 
scheduled available seat miles (ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva 
Viva is Mexico’s ultra-low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of September 2025 it operates 
one of the youngest aircraft fleets in America. With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to 
fly, Viva has democratized the airline industry with one of the lowest fares in Mexico and one of the
