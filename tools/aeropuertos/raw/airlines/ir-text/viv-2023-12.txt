Passengers increased 13.7%, reaching an 84.1% Load Factor  
 
Mexico City, January 9, 2024 – Viva Aerobus announced today its December 2023 
preliminary traffic results . Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 13.7% 
compared to December 2022, reaching 2.3 million passengers, driven by an 
increase of 13.4% and 15.7% in domestic and international traffic, respectively. Load 
factors reached 84.1% compared to 84.4% in 2022, with a load factor mix of 86.4% 
and 74.3% in domestic and international, respectively. 
Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity  in available seat miles (ASMs) by 15.5% 
compared to  December 2022, reflecting an increase of 14.5% and 20.2% in 
domestic and international, respectively. 
“We closed out 2023 with solid traffic and demand environment . Demand trends 
remain robust for the start of 2024, following the favorable market dynamics seen in 
past months. With the second phase of the Pratt & Whitney GTF preventive engine 
inspections starting in early 2024, we remain focused on running a reliable operation 
and continuing our disciplined approach toward capacity deployment.” said Juan 
Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva Aerobus

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents  the number of seats available for passengers  
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor: Represents the number of miles flown by passenger (RPMs) divided by available seat miles 
(ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva Aerobus 
Viva Aerobus is Mexico’s ultra-low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of December 2023 it 
operates one of the youngest Latin American fleets with 84 Airbus: 48 Airbus A320 and 36 Airbus A321. 
With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva Aerobus has democratized the airline 
industry with one of the lowest fares in Mexico and one of the lowest cost structures in America, making 
their flights the best value offer. For more information please visit: http://www.vivaaerobus.com/en
