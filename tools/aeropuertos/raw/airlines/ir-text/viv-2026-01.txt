Passengers increased 3.7%, reaching an 83.5% load factor 
 
Mexico City, February 11, 2026 – Viva announced today its January 2026 preliminary 
traffic results. Viva ’s passenger traffic increased  3.7% compared to January 2025, 
reaching 2.5 million passengers. 
Viva grew its total capacity in available seat miles (ASMs) by 1.9% compared to January 
2025, reflecting a 2.6% increase in domestic capacity and a 0.5% decrease in 
international capacity. Load factors stood at 83.5% compared to 85.4% in 2025, with a 
load factor of 84.6% and 79.0% in domestic and international, respectively. 
“Demand remained resilient at the start of the year, with passenger  volumes up 3.7%, 
driven by  5.0% domestic growth . Our load factor of 83.5% reflect s seasonal 
normalization following December’s operational adjustments , including weather -
related disruptions and international capacity reallocation . Despite these temporary 
factors and the ongoing Pratt & Whitney engine groundings, our focus remains on 
operational stability and proactive capacity management.” said Juan Carlos Zuazua, 
CEO of Viva.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents the number of seats available for passengers 
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor:  Represents the number of miles flown by scheduled passengers (RPMs) divided by 
scheduled available seat miles (ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva 
Viva is a Mexican ultra-low-cost airline that provides air transport services across Mexico, United States, 
and other Latin American countries. Founded in 2006, Viva operates one of the youngest aircraft fleets 
in America. With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva is focused on democratizing 
air travel by offering low fares and a low cost service. For more information please visit:
