Passengers increased 21.7%, reaching an 84.4% Load Factor  
 
Mexico City, January 6, 2023 – Viva Aerobus announced today its December 2022 
preliminary traffic results . Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 21.7% to 2.02 
million passengers compared to December 2021, driven by an increase of 24.6% 
and 2.6% in domestic and international traffic, respectively. Load factors  reached 
84.4% compared to  84.3% in  2021, reflecting an 86.6% and 74.4% load factor in 
domestic and international, respectively. 
Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity  in available seat miles (ASMs) by 20.4% 
compared to December 2021, reflecting an increase of 23.2% and 9.3% in domestic 
and international, respectively. 
Year-to-date (January-December), Viva’s traffic reached 20.70 million passengers, 
a 35.6% increase compared to 2021, with load factors increasing 2.2 p.p. to 85.8%.  
“We saw strong demand for December and healthy passenger loads across all our 
markets. We got delivery of  our last A321neo of the year, totaling a fleet of 69  
aircraft, one of the youngest fleets in Latin America with 5.2 years of age. Our Gente 
Viva did an amazing job of transporting over 2  million passengers, despite the 
operational challenges due to inclement weather across our network. Lastly, we are 
very excited with the performance of our new Loyalty program , Doters, which has 
now more than 1.3 million members in less than 5 months”, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, 
CEO of Viva Aerobus

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents  the number of seats available for passengers  
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor: Represents the number of miles flown by passenger (RPMs) divided by available seat miles 
(ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva Aerobus 
Viva Aerobus is Mexico’s ultra-low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of December 2022 it 
operates one of the youngest Latin American fleets with 69 Airbus: 43 Airbus A320 and 26 Airbus A321. 
With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva Aerobus has democratized the airline 
industry with one of the lowest fares in Mexico and one of the lowest cost structure in America, making 
their flights the best value offer. For more information please visit: http://www.vivaaerobus.com/en
