 
VIVA AEROBUS REPORTS MONTHLY PASSENGER TRAFFIC: 1.22 M Pax; + 89.4% 
vs Feb. 21 / LF: 84.3% 
Mexico City, March 7, 2022.- Viva Aerobus announced today its February 2022 preliminary traffic results. 
This month, Viva Aerobus traffic increased 89.4% vs February 2021, totaling 1.22 million passengers. Load 
factor reached 84.3%; an increase of 7.6 points from February 2021. 
 
Viva´s domestic traffic was over 1.08 million passengers, a 76.8% increase as compared to February 2021, 
with an 88% load factor. International traffic was more than 135 thousand passengers, a 341.3% growth vs 
February 2021, with a 69.1% load factor. 
 
In February 2022, passenger demand, measured in Revenue Passenger Miles (RPMs) in the domestic and 
international markets increased 74.8% and 592.4%, respectively, as compared to February 2021. Total 
demand (RPMs) was up 98.6% YoY. 
 
As a result, Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity, measured in available seat miles (ASMs), by 80.6% vs 
February 2021. Domestically, Viva grew its capacity by 55.2% and by 455.7% internationally. 
 
“Demand trends persisted throughout the month, despite the Omicron variant widespread in Mexic o. We’ll 
continue to grow our business responsibly deploying capacity in existing markets and opening new routes; 
during the first two months we’ve announced non-stop service between Cancun–Medellin, Medellin–Mexico 
and Mexico–Nuevo Laredo, said Juan Carlos Zuazua, CEO of Viva Aerobus.

Glossary 
• Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the 
report, regardless of whether they flew or not. 
• ASM (Available Seat Miles): the number of seats available for passengers multiplied by the 
number of miles the seats are flown. They are calculated by multiplying the available seats for a 
given aircraft by the number of miles that aircraft will travel for a given flight. 
• RPM (Revenue Passenger Miles): the number of miles flown by passengers. 
• Load Factor: RPMs divided by ASMs and expressed as a percentage.
