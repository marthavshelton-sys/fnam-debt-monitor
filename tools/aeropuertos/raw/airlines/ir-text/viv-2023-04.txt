Passengers increased 20.8%, reaching an 87.4% Load Factor  
 
Mexico City, May 5, 2023 – Viva Aerobus announced today its April 2023 preliminary 
traffic results. Viva Aerobus’ passenger traffic increased 20.8% compared to April 
2022, reaching 2.03 million passengers, driven by an increase of 21.4% and 15.2% in 
domestic and international traffic, respectively . Load factors  reached 87.4% 
compared to 84.6% in 2022, with a load factor mix of 88.7% and 81.1% in domestic 
and international, respectively. 
Viva Aerobus grew its total capacity  in available seat miles (ASMs) by 16.4% 
compared to April 2022, reflecting an increase of 19.6% and 3.4% in domestic and 
international, respectively. 
“In April, we saw healthy load factors, reflecting a solid demand environment in both 
the domestic and international markets. In line with our focus on strengthening our 
network, we announced the opening of four new routes in the New Mexico City 
Airport – AIFA, increasing our offering from seven to eleven routes. Additionally, we 
are excited about our agreement to buy one million liters from leading Sustainable 
Aviation Fuel ( SAF) producer Neste . Starting the second half of the year, our 
departing flights from the Los Angeles Airport (LAX) to Guadalajara, Mexico City 
(MEX), and Monterrey, will be operated with a SAF blend.” said Juan Carlos Zuazua, 
CEO of Viva Aerobus.

Glossary 
Passengers: Customers who purchased their plane ticket to fly during the month referred in the report, 
regardless of whether they flew or not. 
ASMs: Stands for "available seat miles" and represents  the number of seats available for passengers  
multiplied by the number of miles. 
Load Factor: Represents the number of miles flown by passenger (RPMs) divided by available seat miles 
(ASMs) and expressed as a percentage. 
RPMs: Stands for “revenue passenger miles” and represents the number of miles flown by passengers. 
 
About Viva Aerobus 
Viva Aerobus is Mexico’s ultra -low-cost airline. It started operations in 2006 and as of April 2023 it 
operates one of the youngest Latin American fleets with 70 Airbus: 42 Airbus A320 and 28 Airbus A321. 
With a clear vision to give all people the opportunity to fly, Viva Aerobus has democratized the airline 
industry with one of the lowest fares in Mexico and one of the lowest cost structures in America, making 
their flights the best value offer. For more information please visit: http://www.vivaaerobus.com/en
