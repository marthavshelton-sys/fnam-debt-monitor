GENTERA
3Q26 RESULTS
Mexico City, Mexico – October 21, 2026 – GENTERA S.A.B. de C.V. (“GENTERA” or “the Company”)
(BMV: GENTERA*) announced today non-audited consolidated financial results for the third quarter ended
September 30, 2026. Figures were prepared in accordance with requirements from the National Banking and
Securities Commission (CNBV) and are expressed in nominal Mexican pesos (Ps.).
3Q26 Highlights:
Loan Portfolio stood at Ps. 95,220 million, representing an 8.5% annual growth, and servicing a
record number of 6.87 million people.
• Total Loan Portfolio broken down by subsidiary was:
• Banco Compartamos S.A., I.B.M. (Mexico) (Banco Compartamos) loan portfolio reached Ps.
61,912 million, a 6.5% growth compared to 3Q25.
• Compartamos Banco, S.A. Peru stood at Ps. 26,431 million, an 11.6% growth compared
to 3Q25 (in local currency loan portfolio grew 11.9% in its annual comparison).
• ConCrédito (Mexico) achieved a record loan portfolio of Ps. 6,878 million, a 14.4% growth
compared to 3Q25.
•
Net income for 3Q26 stood at Ps. 2,342 million, representing an 8.6% increase compared to Ps.
2,156 million reached in 3Q25.
• Controlling Company participation in 3Q26 reported a Net Income of Ps. 2,261 million, a | 7.7%
growth compared to Ps. 2,099 million reported in 3Q25.
•
Cash and investments in financial instruments in GENTERA stood at Ps. 20,787 million in
3Q26, compared to Ps. 15,318 million in 3Q25, featuring sound liquidity levels at its different
subsidiaries:
• Banco Compartamos stood at Ps. 10,997 million.
• Compartamos Banco Peru stood at Ps. 5,474 million.
•
ConCrédito stood at Ps. 942 million.
•
Capital / Total Assets in GENTERA for 3Q26 stood at 31.5%.
•
Capital Adequacy Ratio (ICAP), 34.2% in Banco Compartamos.
•
Solvency Ratio, 23.6% in Compartamos Banco Peru.
• Capital/Total Assets at 50.6% in ConCrédito.
• Controlling ROE in 3Q26 stood at 23.8% compared to 25.5% reached in 3Q25; ROE
considering non-controlling interest stood at 24.5% in 3Q26 compared to 26.0% in 3Q25.
•
ROA in 3Q26 reached 7.6%, a similar ratio compared to 7.7% reached in 3Q25.
•
Loan Portfolio with credit risk stage 3 for 3Q26 stood at 4.57%, compared to NPL at 3.43% in
3Q25 and 4.04% in 2Q26. Coverage Ratio for 3Q26 stood at 206.4%.
• According to the dividend approved on April 10, 2026, at Gentera’s Annual Shareholders
Meeting, the second and final payment amounting ~Ps. 1,642.9 million will be paid on November
13, 2026.

Comments from Mr. Enrique Majós, GENTERA’s CEO:
At GENTERA, we concluded the third quarter of 2026 with solid results, which reflect the resilience of our
business model, the discipline with which we execute our strategy, as well as our clear commitment to continue
creating Total Value for clients, employees, investors, and communities. In this sense, although we are proud
to have reached a record of 6.87 million people served across Mexico and Peru, we cannot ignore the fact that
the current context requires us to exercise greater caution in our pace of growth.
At the end of 3Q26, the Total Loan Portfolio amounted to Ps. 95,220 million, 8.5% higher than a year ago and
a new high for the Company. Net Income for the quarter totaled Ps. 2,342 million (+8.6% YoY) and, for the
first nine months of the year, reached Ps. 7,189 million (+10.9% YoY). With ROE at 24.5%, ROA at 7.6%,
and a Capital / Total Assets ratio of 31.5% for 3Q26; We maintain a combination of strong profitability and
capital strength that gives us room to act prudently.
Under this context, we are aware that the NPL ratio, at 4.57%, stands above the levels observed in previous
quarters. For this reason, we have decided to grow more cautiously, especially in our Mexican operation, and
to focus on containing this indicator. The measures we have put in place since the second quarter, together
with our deep knowledge of the market, are supporting us in this effort.
Regarding our subsidiaries, ATERNA and YASTÁS continued to complement the offering we make available to
our clients. ATERNA concluded September with 17.1 million active insurance policies, compared to 16.2 million
a year earlier, while YASTÁS carried out ~9.4 million operations, around 22% more than in 3Q25, through its
5,800 affiliates, bringing financial services closer to localities where banking infrastructure is scarce.
Today, we are prioritizing prudence over strong growth. Once we have greater evidence that the quality of
the portfolio has stabilized, we will resume the double-digit growth that characterized GENTERA in previous
years. In the meantime, we will continue operating as a well-capitalized institution with sound profitability
metrics and at same time aligned to our purpose of serving more clients and fostering greater financial
inclusion in Mexico and Peru.

Net Interest Margin
GENTERA’s Net Interest Margin (NIM) for the third quarter of 2026 stood at 42.1%, which is similar to
the 42.2% reached in 3Q25. The driver of this movement is attributed to the interest income generated in
the period due to the high single-digit loan portfolio growth of Gentera; and due to the improvements
(contraction) in the Interest Expense Line (Considering Financing Expenses and Expenses linked to credit
origination and the implicit interest related to leasing agreements) compared to previous quarters.
Note: It is important to bear in mind the impact that the size of productive assets (Cash & Investments
and Average Loan Portfolio) have at the time this ratio is computed (Considering average numbers vs.
end of period numbers), and the effect that the evolution of each subsidiary and credit methodology had in
the interest income generation and thus in margins.
Provisions for loan losses
Provisions for loan losses reached Ps. 3,753 million during the quarter. This figure increased by Ps | 904
million, or 31.7%, when compared to 3Q25, and a 9.2% increase compared to 2Q26. In its annual comparison,
the movements are explained mainly by the provisions required in Banco Compartamos Mexico portfolio
according to its mix and considering the current levels of NPL in its loan portfolio, at the same time it reflects
the provisions required by Banco Compartamos Peru and ConCrédito’s portfolio according to the solid | 11.6%
and 14.4% loan growth respectively experienced in these subsidiaries during the period.
Cost of Risk for 3Q26 amounted to 15.8%, compared to 13.3% recorded in 3Q25. Cost of risk for the nine-
