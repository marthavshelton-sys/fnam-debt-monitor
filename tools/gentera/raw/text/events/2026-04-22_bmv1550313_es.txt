GENTERA
REPORTS 1Q26 RESULTS
Mexico City, Mexico – April 22, 2026 – GENTERA S.A.B. de C.V. (“GENTERA” or “the Company”)
(BMV: GENTERA*) announced today non-audited consolidated financial results for the first quarter ended
March 31, 2026. Figures were prepared in accordance with requirements from the National Banking and
Securities Commission (CNBV) and are expressed in nominal Mexican pesos (Ps.).
1Q26 Highlights:
Loan Portfolio stood at Ps. 94,863 million, representing a 14.7% annual growth, and
servicing a record number of 6.6 million people.
• Total Loan Portfolio reached a record of Ps. 94,863 million. Total Loan Portfolio broken down by
subsidiary was:
•
Banco Compartamos S.A., I.B.M. (Mexico) (Banco Compartamos) loan portfolio reached a record
level at Ps. 63,487 million, an 18.8% growth compared to 1Q25.
•
Compartamos Banco, S.A. Perú stood at Ps. 25,309 million, a 5.8% growth compared to 1Q25
(in local currency loan portfolio grew 14.9% in its annual comparison).
• ConCrédito (Mexico) achieved a record loan portfolio of Ps. 6,066 million, a 14.1% growth
compared to 1Q25.
• Net income for 1Q26 stood at a record level of Ps. 2,494 million, representing a 12.3% increase
compared to Ps. 2,221 million reached in 1Q25.
• Controlling Company participation in 1Q26 reported a Net Income of Ps. 2,429 million, a
15.3% growth compared to Ps. 2,106 million reported in 1Q25.
• Cash and investments in financial instruments in GENTERA stood at Ps. 17,457 million in 1Q26,
compared to Ps. 15,670 million in 1Q25, featuring sound liquidity levels at its different subsidiaries:
•
Banco Compartamos stood at Ps. 8,336 million.
•
Compartamos Banco Perú stood at Ps. 4,821 million.
• ConCrédito stood at Ps. 1,687 million.
• Capital / Total Assets in GENTERA for 1Q26 stood at 31.3%.
• Capital Adequacy Ratio (ICAP), 32.3% in Banco Compartamos.
•
Solvency Ratio, 21.7% in Compartamos Banco Perú.
•
Capital/Total Assets at 46.6% in ConCrédito.
•
Controlling ROE in 1Q26 stood at 26.5% compared to 26.3% reached in 1Q25; ROE considering
non-controlling interest stood at 26.9% in 1Q26 compared to 25.8% in 1Q25.
• ROA in 1Q26 reached 8.2%, an improvement compared to 7.0% reached in 4Q25, and a similar ratio
compared to 8.3% reached in 1Q25.
• On April 10, 2026, at GENTERA’s Annual Shareholders’ Meeting a dividend payment equivalent
to 40% of the Net Income generated in GENTERA's participation in 2025 results was approved.

Comments from Mr. Enrique Majós, GENTERA’s CEO:
At GENTERA, we are pleased to report a solid beginning to 2026, reflecting the consistent execution of our strategy, the
strength of our business model, and the commitment of our team to continue generating Total Value for all our stakeholders,
while supporting our clients in achieving their goals and improving their well-being. Our modernization and transformation
efforts continue to support strong operational and financial performance, while helping us deepen our positive impact in the
communities we serve.
In 1Q26, our Total Loan Portfolio reached a new record of Ps. 94,863 million, representing a 14.7% year-over-year increase,
while serving a record 6.6 million people across Mexico and Peru. Net Income also reached a new all-time high for a quarter
at Ps. 2,494 million, a 12.3% increase compared to 1Q25. Profitability remained strong, with ROE standing at 26.9% and
ROA at 8.2%, reflecting the efficiency of our operation.
Our subsidiaries continued contributing to the generation of economic, social, and human value. ATERNA concluded the
quarter with 16.2 million active insurance policies, reinforcing its role in protecting the well-being of millions of families.
Meanwhile, YASTÁS executed 8.6 million operations through a network of 5,648 affiliates, expanding access to financial
services in areas with limited banking infrastructure.
At the same time, we remain focused on maintaining sound portfolio quality. While the level of loans in credit risk stage | 3
increased during the quarter, it remains within the expected range for the current portfolio mix, and we are implementing
actions, particularly in Mexico, to control and improve this indicator with the objective of preserving a prudent and healthy
asset quality.
Looking ahead, we remain confident in our ability to continue growing in a solid and sustainable manner, supported by the
modernization of our business model, disciplined execution, and prudent risk management. We will continue working to
strengthen our capabilities, improve customer experience, and expand financial inclusion, while creating lasting value for
our clients, employees, investors, and the communities we serve.

Net Interest Margin
GENTERA’s Net Interest Margin (NIM) for the first quarter of 2026 stood at 40.0%, which is slightly
above the 39.5% reached in 1Q25. The driver of this movement is attributed to the interest income generated
in the period due to the strong loan portfolio performance of Banco Compartamos México and Perú, and
ConCrédito; and due to the smaller growth in the Interest Expense Line (Considering Financing Expenses and
Expenses linked to credit origination and the implicit interest related to leasing agreements) compared to
previous quarters.
Note: It is important to bear in mind the impact that the size of productive assets (Cash & Investments
and Average Loan Portfolio) have at the time this ratio is computed (Considering average numbers vs.
end of period numbers), and the effect that the evolution of each credit methodology had in the interest
income generation and thus in margins.

mix and ConCrédito’s portfolio due to the solid 18.8% and 14.1% loan growth respectively experienced in
these subsidiaries during the period.

Credit Quality (Loan Portfolio with credit risk stage 3/Loan Portfolio)
Consolidated non-performing loans (Loan Portfolio with credit risk stage 3), considering the three
financial subsidiaries, reached 4.13% in 1Q26, a higher level compared to 3.73% recorded in 1Q25, and
to the 3.83% NPL registered in 4Q25. While recognizing that the NPL level recorded in 1Q26 was higher
compared to the previous quarters, this level is still within the consolidated NPL range expected for this year,
which is projected be moving around 4.0% according to the portfolio mix. Worth highlighting is the fact that
we are implementing actions in Mexico to control it and maintaining an adequate and healthy level.
Banco Compartamos’ policy is to write-off loans at 180 days behind schedule. As we have signaled before,
GENTERA acquired vast knowledge and experience in originating and monitoring credits in recent years, and
at the same time it is now using in a more active manner all the data that it has generated in the past years
to become a more efficient financial institution.
