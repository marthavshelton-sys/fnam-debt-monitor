GENTERA Announces Successful Placement of Four Social Bonds
COMPART 26S, COMPART 26-2S, COMPART 26-3S, and COMPART 26-4S
Issued by its Subsidiary Banco Compartamos
Mexico City, Mexico, as of August 25, 2026 - Gentera, S.A.B. de C.V. (GENTERA) (BMV:
GENTERA *) informs that its subsidiary in Mexico, Banco Compartamos S.A.
I.B.M.,(“Banco Compartamos” or “the Institution”) announces that successfully issued
the amount of Ps. 4,000 million in the local debt market through four public issuances of Bank
Certificates of Ps. 1,000 million each (COMPART 26S, COMPART 26-2S, COMPART 26-3S, and
COMPART 26-4S) (the “Issuances”), all issued under a Social Bond framework.
COMPART 26S issuance has a 5-year term and an interest rate of TIIE Fondeo + 105 basis
points; COMPART 26-2S has a 6-year term and an interest rate of TIIE Fondeo + 110 basis
points; COMPART 26-3S has a 7-year term with a fixed interest rate of 10.05%; and
COMPART 26-4S has a 4.5-year term and an interest rate of TIIE Fondeo + 100 basis points.
The issuances received credit ratings of “AA.mx” from Moody’s Local and “mxAA” from S&P
Global Ratings.
Through these issuances, Banco Compartamos reinforces its long-term funding strategy and
strengthens its capacity to continue expanding access to financial services. In addition, their
social bond designation reinforces the Institution’s commitment to financial inclusion and the
development of the communities it serves, contributing to the creation of Total Value for its
customers, employees, investors, and other key stakeholders.

Descripción de la Compañía
GENTERA, S.A.B. de C.V. (Antes Compartamos, S.A.B. de C.V.) es una compañía tenedora de acciones, cuyo objetivo es promover,
organizar y administrar, todo tipo de compañías, nacionales e internacionales, sujeto a sus políticas de inversión. GENTERA se fundó
en la Ciudad de México, Distrito Federal en 2010. Las acciones de GENTERA comenzaron a cotizar en la Bolsa Mexicana de Valores,
S.A.B. de C.V. el 24 de diciembre de 2010 bajo la clave de cotización COMPARC*; Actualizando la clave de cotización el 2 de enero de
2014 a GENTERA*.
