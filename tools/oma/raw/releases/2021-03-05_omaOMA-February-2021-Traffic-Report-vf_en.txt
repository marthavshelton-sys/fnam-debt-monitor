OMA reports a 53.9% decrease in
February 2021 passenger traffic
Mexico City, Mexico, March 5, 2021—Mexican airport operator Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte, S.A.B.
de C.V., known as OMA (NASDAQ: OMAB; BMV: OMA), reports that terminal passenger traffic at its 13 airports
decreased 53.9% in February 2021, as compared to February 2020. Domestic traffic decreased 51.0%, and
international traffic decreased 70.8%. Passenger traffic decrease reflects the impact on demand caused by
COVID-19. Decreases in passenger traffic reflect the impact on demand caused by COVID-19, as well as
restrictions to international flight operations to the United States and flight suspensions in Canada, which
mainly affected international passenger traffic performance in Monterrey, Mazatlán and Zihuatanejo.

About OMA
Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte, S.A.B. de C.V., known as OMA, operates 13 international airports in nine
states of central and northern Mexico. OMA’s airports serve Monterrey, Mexico’s third largest metropolitan area,
the tourist destinations of Acapulco, Mazatlán, and Zihuatanejo, and nine other regional centers and border cities.
OMA also operates the NH Collection Hotel inside Terminal 2 of the Mexico City airport and the Hilton Garden Inn at
the Monterrey airport. OMA employs over 1,000 persons in order to offer passengers and clients airport and
commercial services in facilities. OMA is listed on the Mexican Stock Exchange (OMA) and on the NASDAQ Global
Select Market (OMAB). For more information, visit:
