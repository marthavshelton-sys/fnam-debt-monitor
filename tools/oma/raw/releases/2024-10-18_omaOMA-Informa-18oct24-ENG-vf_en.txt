OMA Informs on Cybersecurity Breach
Mexico City, Mexico, October 18, 2024— Mexican airport operator Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte,
S.A.B. de C.V., known as OMA (NASDAQ: OMAB; BMV: OMA), has identified a recent cybersecurity breach
involving unauthorized access to certain IT systems and information. The Company promptly activated its
established security protocols and response measures.
OMA’s IT team, in collaboration with external cybersecurity experts, is actively investigating the incident to
determine its scope and ensure the protection of our systems’ integrity, confidentiality, and availability. Our
operations are currently running without interruption through alternative and backup systems.
At this time, there has been no material adverse effect on the Company’s operations, financial performance,
or condition, which assessment will be monitored on an ongoing basis until the situation has been fully
resolved. A forensic assessment is currently underway to understand the extent of the cybersecurity incident.

About OMA
Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte, S.A.B. de C.V., known as OMA, operates 13 international airports in nine states
of central and northern Mexico. OMA’s airports serve Monterrey, Mexico’s third largest metropolitan area, the tourist
destinations of Acapulco, Mazatlán, and Zihuatanejo, and nine other regional centers and border cities. OMA also
operates the NH Collection Hotel inside Terminal 2 of the Mexico City airport and the Hilton Garden Inn at the
Monterrey airport. OMA employs over 1,200 persons in order to offer passengers and clients airport and commercial
services in facilities. OMA is listed on the Mexican Stock Exchange (OMA) and on the NASDAQ Global Select Market
(OMAB). For more information, visit:
