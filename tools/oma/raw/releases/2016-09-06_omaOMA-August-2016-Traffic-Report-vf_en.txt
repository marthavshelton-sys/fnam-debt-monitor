OMA reports a 10.7% increase in August
2016 passenger traffic
Monterrey, Mexico, September 6, 2016—Mexican airport operator Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte,
S.A.B. de C.V., known as OMA (NASDAQ: OMAB; BMV: OMA), reports that terminal passenger traffic at its | 13
airports increased 10.7% in August 2016, as compared to August 2015. Domestic traffic increased 13.0%, and
international traffic decreased 5.6%.
Of total August traffic, 98.4% was commercial aviation and 1.6% was general aviation.

Domestic traffic increased in nine airports in August. The most noteworthy increases were in Monterrey
(+11.8%; +82,339 passengers), Culiacán (+19.9%; +24,963), Ciudad Juárez (+31.4%; +24,609), Chihuahua
(+19.9%; +19,238), and Torreón (+23.5%; +10,479). Monterrey traffic increased principally because of higher
traffic volumes on the Guadalajara, Tijuana and Mexico City routes. Culiacán traffic increased as a result of
higher volumes on its Tijuana and Guadalajara routes. Ciudad Juárez, Chihuahua, and Torreón increased traffic
principally on their Mexico City routes.
TAR opened the Mazatlán – Torreón route in August.
International traffic increased in six airports in August. Traffic increased most significantly in Durango
(+121.1%; +3,349 passengers), principally because of higher volumes on its Chicago and Los Angeles routes.
Monterrey (-9.6%; -11,920 passengers) recorded the largest decrease, principally because of reduced traffic on
its Dallas and Las Vegas routes.
The number of flight operations (takeoffs and landings) in August increased 2.9%. The number of domestic
operations increased 3.5%, and international operations decreased 0.8%.

About OMA
Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte, S.A.B. de C.V., known as OMA, operates 13 international airports in nine
states of central and northern Mexico. OMA’s airports serve Monterrey, Mexico’s third largest metropolitan area,
the tourist destinations of Acapulco, Mazatlán, and Zihuatanejo, and nine other regional centers and border cities.
OMA also operates the NH Collection hotel inside Terminal 2 of the Mexico City airport and the Hilton Garden Inn at
the Monterrey airport. OMA employs over 1,000 persons in order to offer passengers and clients airport and
commercial services in facilities that comply with all applicable international safety, security, and ISO 9001:2008
environmental standards. OMA’s strategic shareholder members are ICA, Mexico’s largest engineering,
procurement, and construction company, and Aéroports de Paris Management, subsidiary of Aéroports de Paris,
the second largest European airports operator. OMA is listed on the Mexican Stock Exchange (OMA) and on the
NASDAQ Global Select Market (OMAB). For more information, visit:
