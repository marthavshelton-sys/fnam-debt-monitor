OMA Provides Additional Information on
Cybersecurity Incident
Mexico City, Mexico, November 5, 2024— Mexican airport operator Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte,
S.A.B. de C.V., known as OMA (NASDAQ: OMAB; BMV: OMA) reports that, following the press release of
October 18, 2024, the cybersecurity incident consisted of ransomware that encrypted some of our files and
systems and exfiltration of certain information. As we have continued the investigation and the
implementation of our security and contingency protocols, we have worked on restoring systems using
backups, notifying the relevant authorities, and implementing mitigation measures.
Regarding information that has been identified as compromised related to certain of our business customers,
suppliers and employees, and which the attacker has purportedly released, we are investigating and taking
measures to inform them about the incident as required.
We are in process of further strengthening our security response protocols, policies and procedures, and our
ability to detect and respond to suspected incidents.
We have not acceded to any payment demands from the attackers, nor have we suffered any material adverse
effect on the Company's operations, results or financial position, which will continue to be assessed and
reported as appropriate.

About OMA
Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte, S.A.B. de C.V., known as OMA, operates 13 international airports in nine states
of central and northern Mexico. OMA’s airports serve Monterrey, Mexico’s third largest metropolitan area, the tourist
destinations of Acapulco, Mazatlán, and Zihuatanejo, and nine other regional centers and border cities. OMA also
operates the NH Collection Hotel inside Terminal 2 of the Mexico City airport and the Hilton Garden Inn at the
Monterrey airport. OMA employs over 1,200 persons in order to offer passengers and clients airport and commercial
services in facilities. OMA is listed on the Mexican Stock Exchange (OMA) and on the NASDAQ Global Select Market
(OMAB). For more information, visit:
