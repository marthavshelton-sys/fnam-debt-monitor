OMA Reports on the Publication of
VINCI SA’s 2025 Annual Results
Mexico City, Mexico, February 6, 2026.– Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte, S.A.B. de C.V. (BMV: OMA;
NASDAQ: OMAB) informs that today VINCI SA, the parent company of VINCI Airports SAS, which holds an
indirect equity interest of 29.99% in OMA’s shares, published its annual results for the fiscal year 2025. Such
results include, among other information, certain financial figures related to OMA.
The information concerning OMA included in VINCI SA’s 2025 annual results is based on OMA’s unaudited
financial information and includes, among other items, estimates of OMA’s financial position and certain
accounting recognitions made at the VINCI SA level related to fair value remeasurement.
VINCI SA’s 2025 annual results are available at the following link: https://www.vinci.com/en/finance/investors

About OMA
Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte, S.A.B. de C.V., known as OMA, operates 13 international airports in nine states of central and northern Mexico.
OMA’s airports serve Monterrey, Mexico’s third largest metropolitan area, the tourist destinations of Acapulco, Mazatlán, and Zihuatanejo, and nine
other regional centers and border cities. OMA also operates the NH Collection Hotel inside Terminal 2 of the Mexico City airport and the Hilton Garden
Inn at the Monterrey airport. OMA employs over 1,200 persons in order to offer passengers and clients airport and commercial services in facilities.
OMA is listed on the Mexican Stock Exchange (OMA) and on the NASDAQ Global Select Market (OMAB). Since December 2022, OMA is part of VINCI
Airports, the world´s leading private airport operator.
