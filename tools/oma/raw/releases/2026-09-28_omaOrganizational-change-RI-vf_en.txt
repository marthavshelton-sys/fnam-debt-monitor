OMA Announces Change in Investor Relation Officer
Mexico City, Mexico, September 28, 2026—Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte, S.A.B. de C.V., known as
OMA (NASDAQ: OMAB; BMV: OMA) (“OMA” or the “Company”) informs that, effective September | 30, | 2026,
Vanessa Paola Velazco Barraza will assume the role of Investor Relations Officer, succeeding Luis Emmanuel
Camacho Thierry.
Ms. Velazco previously worked at OMA from 2021 to 2024 in Investor Relations and Financial Planning and has
recently rejoined the Company. She has solid experience in investor relations and corporate finance.
Mr. Camacho will leave his position at OMA to assume a new position within the VINCI Airports network. OMA
thanks Emmanuel for his contributions to the Company and wishes him success in his new responsibilities.

About OMA
Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte, S.A.B. de C.V., known as OMA, operates 13 international airports in nine states of central and northern Mexico.
OMA’s airports serve Monterrey, Mexico’s third largest metropolitan area, the tourist destinations of Acapulco, Mazatlán, and Zihuatanejo, and nine
other regional centers and border cities. OMA also operates the NH Collection Hotel inside Terminal 2 of the Mexico City airport and the Hilton Garden
Inn at the Monterrey airport. OMA employs over 1,200 people to offer passengers and clients airport and commercial services in facilities. OMA is listed
on the Mexican Stock Exchange (OMA) and on the NASDAQ Global Select Market (OMAB). Since December 2022, OMA is part of VINCI Airports, the
world´s leading private airport operator.
