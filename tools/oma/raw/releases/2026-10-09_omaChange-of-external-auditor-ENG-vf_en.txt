OMA announces external auditor change
Mexico City, Mexico, October 9, 2026—Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte, S.A.B. de C.V., known as OMA
(NASDAQ: OMAB; BMV: OMA) announces that it has appointed Mancera, S.C. (“EY”), member of Ernst &
Young Global Limited, as the firm responsible for providing external audit services for OMA’s basic financial
statements and those of its subsidiaries, effective for fiscal year 2026, replacing Galaz, Yamazaki, Ruiz Urquiza,
S.C. (“Deloitte”).
The replacement of the external auditor is part of the Issuer’s efforts to promote continuous improvement and
strengthen its audit processes, in line with corporate governance best practices.
The Board of Directors, its Audit Committee, together with OMA’s Management, acknowledge and express
their gratitude to Galaz, Yamazaki, Ruiz Urquiza, S.C. for its commitment, dedication, and professionalism
throughout the years in which it provided services as the Issuer’s independent external auditor.

About OMA
Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte, S.A.B. de C.V., known as OMA, operates 13 international airports in nine states of central and northern Mexico.
OMA’s airports serve Monterrey, Mexico’s third largest metropolitan area, the tourist destinations of Acapulco, Mazatlán, and Zihuatanejo, and nine
other regional centers and border cities. OMA also operates the NH Collection Hotel inside Terminal 2 of the Mexico City airport and the Hilton Garden
Inn at the Monterrey airport. OMA employs over 1,200 people to offer passengers and clients airport and commercial services in facilities. OMA is listed
on the Mexican Stock Exchange (OMA) and on the NASDAQ Global Select Market (OMAB). Since December 2022, OMA is part of VINCI Airports, the
world´s leading private airport operator.
