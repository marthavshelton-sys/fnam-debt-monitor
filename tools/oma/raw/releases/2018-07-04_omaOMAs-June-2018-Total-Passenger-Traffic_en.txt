OMA reports a 9.1% increase in
June 2018 passenger traffic
 The Monterrey (+9.0%), Culiacán (+24.0%), and Ciudad Juárez (+18.9%)
airports contributed most to traffic growth
Monterrey, Mexico, July 4, 2018—Mexican airport operator Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte, S.A.B. de
C.V., known as OMA (NASDAQ: OMAB; BMV: OMA), reports that terminal passenger traffic at its 13 airports
increased 9.1% in June 2018, as compared to June 2017. Domestic traffic increased 9.9%, and international
traffic increased 3.2%.

* Terminal passengers: includes passengers on the three types of aviation (commercial, charter, and general aviation), and excludes passengers in transit.
During June 2018, domestic traffic increased in ten airports, with the largest increases in:
 Monterrey (+10.3%; +76,253 passengers), with 50% of total domestic traffic, increased traffic
principally because of higher volumes on the Cancún, Mexico City, Tijuana, Guadalajara, Mexicali, and
Veracruz routes.
 Culiacán (+23.7%; +35,021) increased passenger traffic on its Tijuana, Guadalajara, Mexicali, and La
Paz routes.
 Ciudad Juárez (+18.6%; +19,849) increased traffic on its Guadalajara, Cancún, and Monterrey routes.
In June, TAR opened the Ciudad Juárez – Mazatlán, Mazatlán – Ciudad Juárez, Mazatlán – Hermosillo, Tampico
– Querétaro, Torreón – Guadalajara, and Zacatecas – Guadalajara routes; and VivaAerobus opened the
Monterrey – Huatulco, Monterrey – La Paz, and Torreón – Cancún routes.
International traffic grew in ten airports in June. Aeroméxico started flying the Monterrey-Orlando route
during the month.
Of total passenger traffic, 99.0% was commercial, and 1.0% was general aviation.

About OMA
Grupo Aeroportuario del Centro Norte, S.A.B. de C.V., known as OMA, operates 13 international airports in nine
states of central and northern Mexico. OMA’s airports serve Monterrey, Mexico’s third largest metropolitan area,
the tourist destinations of Acapulco, Mazatlán, and Zihuatanejo, and nine other regional centers and border cities.
OMA also operates the NH Collection Hotel inside Terminal 2 of the Mexico City airport and the Hilton Garden Inn at
the Monterrey airport. OMA employs over 1,000 persons in order to offer passengers and clients airport and
commercial services in facilities that comply with all applicable international safety, security, and ISO 9001:2008
environmental standards. OMA is listed on the Mexican Stock Exchange (OMA) and on the NASDAQ Global Select
Market (OMAB). For more information, visit:
